# ABA creative direction: app interpretation

Source: Alex's uploaded `ABA006_Creative_Direction.pdf`, 13 pages. Reviewed both extracted text and the rendered spectrum/moodboards. Alex explicitly selected **Moodboard 02** on 30 October 2026. The document is a design reference, not an instruction to change permissions, publish private strategy, create services or make support commitments.

## What the app should reflect

- **Purpose/mission, pages 2–3:** innovative solutions to client problems, an in-depth process grounded in research/evidence, inspiration and sharing design understanding. In the app this means explaining next steps, making room for context and feedback, and keeping the client's journey personal; it does not introduce an education product or automatic AI/design services.
- **Values, page 4:** loyalty, inspiration, diversity, courage, communication and integrity. Exact value names appear on the welcome screen; the interaction design continues to use clear confirmations, explicit sharing controls and honest verification states.
- **Personality, page 5:** friendly but professional, client-centred and personalised. **Loyalty is key** is the supplied tagline. Use it as the shared footer/signature, separate from the standalone A logo. Use I/me/my in client-facing business copy; do not promise perpetual support, an agreed licence or guaranteed outcomes.
- **Spectrum, page 9:** modern and inclusive, with confident visual presence and a professional/elegant balance. Preserve accessible text, keyboard focus, explicit labels and responsive layouts.
- **Chosen Moodboard 02, page 13:** bold/minimal graphic hierarchy; black/white as the foundation; purple/lime accents; strong display headings, mono supporting labels and readable sans-serif body text. Preserve the previously approved black section bands, lime highlights, standalone A and distinct internal-stage treatment. The canvas now has a quieter neutral-grey/purple tint so the coloured controls retain their prominence.

The earlier moodboard's gradient direction is not the selected reference. Do not turn the working interface into a photographic gallery or replace the supplied A with the example monogram. Photography/logos in the moodboard are reference images and are not bundled as licensed app artwork.

## Typography and provenance

The PDF font metadata identifies Vina Sans, Fira Code, Semplicita Pro and Helvetica Neue. It does not establish a webfont licence for commercial families or mean every illustrative type sample is a supplied font.

The app locally hosts the open-licensed **Vina Sans** display family and **Fira Code** variable mono family. These reflect the bold/mono direction without an external font-service request. Body text and dense controls retain a readable Arial/Helvetica/system sans-serif fallback; no Semplicita Pro or Helvetica Neue font files were extracted from the PDF or purchased.
